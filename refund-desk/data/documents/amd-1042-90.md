## SEC-AMD-1042-90-PARTIES
Parties to this amendment

This is an active signed amendment between Northstar Software, Inc. and Avery Chen for customer NS-CUST-1042, Brightline Analytics. It amends the Lumen Desk subscription agreement for that customer. It is effective on 2026-02-01. It does not name NS-CUST-1184, NS-CUST-2208, or NS-CUST-3310.

## SEC-AMD-1042-90-WINDOW
Amendment refund window

Notwithstanding the Refund Handbook and notwithstanding the base agreement, customer NS-CUST-1042 may request a refund of the initial annual purchase within ninety (90) days after the purchase date. The request is inside this window when elapsed days is less than or equal to 90, and outside it when elapsed days is greater than 90. This amendment applies only to customer NS-CUST-1042.
