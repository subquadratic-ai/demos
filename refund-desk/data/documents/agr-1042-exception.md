## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-PARTIES
Parties

This subscription agreement is between Northstar Software, Inc. and Avery Chen for Brightline Analytics, customer NS-CUST-1042. It covers the Lumen Desk Business annual subscription. It was signed on 2026-02-12 and is effective on that date. Its status is active. It applies only to NS-CUST-1042.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-SERVICES
Services

Northstar will provide Lumen Desk Business, including shared inboxes, assignment rules, and the admin console. Brightline may add seats during the term. Scheduled maintenance is announced on the status page. Uptime credits, if any, are described in the service exhibit and are not refunds of the initial purchase.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-TERM
Term

The initial term starts on the purchase date and runs for one year. The agreement renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives notice before renewal. Ending the term stops the next renewal. This section does not state a refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-FEES
Fees

The initial annual fee is 4800 USD, charged on the purchase date on the account record. Seat additions are billed pro rata. Invoices are due on receipt. Disputed invoice lines must be raised within a reasonable review period. This section does not state a refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-SUPPORT
Support hours

Brightline Analytics may open tickets with Northstar support on weekdays from 08:00 to 20:00 US Eastern time. Severity definitions follow the support exhibit. Response targets are operational goals. They do not create a refund right and they do not state a number of days for a refund.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-DATA
Data processing

Northstar processes account data and ticket content to provide Lumen Desk. Brightline is the controller of its customer content. Subprocessors are listed at the vendor page Northstar maintains. Deletion at the end of the term follows the data processing addendum. This section does not state a refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-INCORP
Refund handbook incorporation

Refunds of subscription purchases are governed by the Northstar Software Refund Handbook except where a clause of this agreement states a different refund window for customer NS-CUST-1042. This section does not itself state a number of days.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-SECURITY
Security obligations

Northstar maintains access controls, encryption in transit, and audit logging for Lumen Desk. Brightline is responsible for its own user administration and for tokens it creates. A security incident is reported through the contact on the order form. This section does not state a refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-WINDOW
Customer-specific refund window

Notwithstanding the Refund Handbook, and notwithstanding any shorter window, customer NS-CUST-1042 may request a refund of the initial annual purchase within sixty (60) days after the purchase date. This clause applies only to customer NS-CUST-1042. It does not apply to any other customer. The request is inside this window when elapsed days is less than or equal to 60, and outside it when elapsed days is greater than 60. This clause is part of the customer's active signed agreement.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-CONF
Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other party's non-public business information confidential and uses it only to perform this agreement. Confidentiality survives for three years after the term. This section does not state a refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-LIABILITY
Limitation of liability

Northstar's aggregate liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid for the current term. Neither party is liable for lost profits or indirect damages. The cap does not apply to fees that this agreement requires Northstar to refund. This section does not state the number of days in the refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-PUBLICITY
Publicity

Northstar may identify Brightline Analytics as a customer by name, but not by logo, unless Brightline agrees in writing. Brightline may withdraw that permission on notice. This section does not address refunds.

## SEC-AGR-1042-EX-SIGN
Signatures

Signed for the customer by Avery Chen, Director of Operations, Brightline Analytics, on 2026-02-12. Signed for Northstar Software by Elena Voss, General Counsel, on 2026-02-12. The signatures apply to every clause in this agreement, including clauses that appear after the fee section.
