## SEC-HIST-CONFERENCE
Summit recap

Northstar held its customer summit in Philadelphia on 14 September 2023. The product sessions covered Lumen Desk macros, Harbor Billing tax exports, and a preview of Quill Analytics sampling controls. The closing dinner was at a restaurant on Market Street. No refund rule was adopted at the summit. These notes are unrelated to any 2026 purchase and name no refund window.
