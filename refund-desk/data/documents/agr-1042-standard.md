## SEC-AGR-1042-STD-PARTIES
Parties

This subscription agreement is between Northstar Software, Inc. and Avery Chen for Brightline Analytics, customer NS-CUST-1042. It covers the Lumen Desk Business annual subscription. It was signed on 2026-02-12 and is effective on that date. Its status is active. It does not name any other customer.

## SEC-AGR-1042-STD-SERVICES
Services

Northstar will provide Lumen Desk Business to Brightline Analytics, including shared inboxes, macros, and the admin console, for the seats listed on the order. Maintenance windows are posted on the status page. This section does not address refunds.

## SEC-AGR-1042-STD-FEES
Fees

The initial annual fee is 4800 USD, charged on the purchase date shown on the account record. Renewal fees are charged on the anniversary of that purchase date. Taxes are extra. This section does not state a refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-STD-WINDOW
Refund window

Customer NS-CUST-1042 may request a refund of the initial purchase within thirty (30) days after the purchase date. This clause is part of the customer's active signed agreement and applies only to NS-CUST-1042. The request is inside this window when elapsed days is less than or equal to 30, and outside it when elapsed days is greater than 30.

## SEC-AGR-1042-STD-LIABILITY
Limitation of liability

Northstar's aggregate liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid for the current term. Neither party is liable for indirect damages. This section does not change the refund window.

## SEC-AGR-1042-STD-SIGN
Signatures

Signed for the customer by Avery Chen, Director of Operations, Brightline Analytics, on 2026-02-12. Signed for Northstar Software by Elena Voss, General Counsel, on 2026-02-12. Both signatures apply to the whole agreement, including the refund window.
