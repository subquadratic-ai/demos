## SEC-TERMS-SCOPE
Scope of the subscription terms

These subscription terms apply to Northstar Software online services ordered on an order form or through the billing portal. They describe billing, cancellation, and the relationship between these terms and the refund handbook. They are company-wide. Version 9.0 is effective on 2026-01-01.

## SEC-TERMS-BILLING
Fees and renewal

Annual subscriptions are billed on the purchase date and renew on the anniversary of that date unless the customer cancels before renewal. A renewal has its own purchase date. Seat additions during a term are billed pro rata and are not a new initial purchase. Prices in an order form control over prices in marketing pages.

## SEC-TERMS-CANCEL
Cancellation

A customer may cancel a subscription effective at the end of the current term. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Cancellation alone does not refund a charge that has already been captured. Refund eligibility is not decided in this section.

## SEC-TERMS-REFUND
Relationship to the refund handbook

Refunds of subscription purchases are described in the Northstar Software Refund Handbook and in the customer's signed agreement or amendment, using the authority order in that handbook. This section does not itself state a number of days.

## SEC-TERMS-DATA
Customer data after cancellation

After a subscription ends, Northstar deletes customer content on the schedule in the data processing addendum, except for records that must be kept for tax or security. Export tools remain available for a short wind-down period after cancellation. That wind-down period is not a refund window.
