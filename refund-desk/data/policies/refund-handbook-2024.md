## SEC-POL-2024-STATUS
Status of the 2024 handbook

This handbook was replaced by version 4.2 on 2026-01-01. Its status is superseded. It is retained so agents can see what rule applied to purchases in 2024 and 2025. It is not authority for a purchase evaluated on a later date.

## SEC-POL-2024-WINDOW
2024 refund window

While this handbook was in effect, the standard refund window was fourteen (14) days from the purchase date. A request was inside that historical window when the elapsed days were less than or equal to 14. This number does not govern purchases evaluated under the current handbook.
