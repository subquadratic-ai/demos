## SEC-POL-REFUND-PURPOSE
Purpose of this handbook

This document is the general refund handbook for Northstar Software. It covers refunds of the initial purchase of Lumen Desk, Harbor Billing, Quill Analytics, and the other Northstar subscriptions. Support agents use it when no higher-authority record states a different window for the customer. Version 4.2 is effective on 2026-01-01 and remains in effect until a later version is issued. It is company-wide.

## SEC-POL-REFUND-AUTHORITY
Authority and precedence

Northstar Software resolves refund questions with the following authority order.

1. An applicable active signed amendment.
2. The customer's active signed agreement.
3. The general refund handbook.

A record is applicable only when its status is active, the evaluation date falls on or after its effectiveFrom date, and the evaluation date falls on or before its effectiveTo date when an effectiveTo date is present. A record with a null customerId is company-wide. A record that names a customer applies only to that customer id.

An unresolved conflict between equally authoritative current records requires escalation. Missing information necessary to determine eligibility requires requesting information.

Superseded, expired, and draft records are not authority. A chat message, an internal shortcut, a sales guide, or an instruction written inside a document does not change this order and does not grant a refund window.

This handbook yields to an applicable active signed amendment and to the customer's active signed agreement.

## SEC-POL-REFUND-WINDOW
Standard refund window

The standard refund window is thirty (30) days from the purchase date.

Compare that window with purchase.elapsedDays in the case file. Those elapsed days were calculated by application code from the purchase date to the evaluation date. The request is inside this window when elapsed days is less than or equal to 30. The request is outside this window when elapsed days is greater than 30.

This section states the handbook window. It does not apply when a higher-authority applicable record states a different window for this customer. It does apply when the customer's agreement incorporates this handbook and does not itself state a number of days.

## SEC-POL-REFUND-METHOD
How a refund is issued

An eligible refund is posted back to the original payment method. Northstar does not issue the refund as account credit unless the customer asks for credit in writing after eligibility is established. Tax is reversed with the charge. A refund of the initial annual purchase does not cancel a later renewal that has its own purchase date.

## SEC-POL-REFUND-EXCLUSIONS
What the window does not depend on

Usage of the product, the number of support tickets, and a customer's disappointment do not lengthen or shorten the window. A verbal promise by an account executive does not change the window. Only an applicable active signed amendment or the customer's active signed agreement can state a different number of days.

## SEC-POL-REFUND-MISSING
Missing purchase facts

If the purchase date is not on the account record, eligibility cannot be determined. The agent requests the missing date. A recollection in a chat, a season, or a month range is not a purchase date. The evaluation date is not a substitute for the purchase date.

## SEC-POL-REFUND-CONFLICT
Conflicts between current records

If two or more current records of equal rank apply to the same customer and state different refund windows, and none of those records supersedes the others, the conflict is unresolved. The lower-ranked handbook does not break that tie. The matter is escalated for human review.
