Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

About the role
We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to work on our payments platform. You
will design and operate high-throughput services that move money reliably, own
critical APIs end to end, and mentor mid-level engineers.

Must-have requirements
- 5+ years building production backend services
- Strong experience with a typed backend language (Go, Java, or TypeScript/Node)
- Designed and operated distributed systems handling real production traffic
- Solid grasp of relational databases (Postgres/MySQL), schema design, and
  transactional correctness
- Experience with asynchronous processing / message queues (Kafka, SQS, etc.)
- Comfortable owning on-call for services you build

Nice to have
- Prior work in payments, fintech, or other high-compliance domains
- Experience with idempotency, reconciliation, and ledgering
- Exposure to Kubernetes and infrastructure-as-code

What you'll do
- Design APIs and data models for new payment flows
- Improve reliability and latency of existing services
- Partner with product and compliance on new features
